Core Mechanics

Three Rules of the Dark

Every system in Shadow of Human is about one thing — the quiet tension between being seen and being free.

01

Control the Shadow

Slide, stretch, and curl along the floor. The shadow obeys light like water obeys gravity — and you must learn to ride it.

02

Escape the Owner

Your human walks through days unaware. Stay tethered too long and you stay forever. Break the line, slip through a doorway, become your own.

03

Manipulate Light

Tip a lamp. Open a curtain. Redirect a beam. Every photon is a platform — and every flick of a switch rewrites the map beneath your feet.
